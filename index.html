The Golden Seed

A Philosoraptors story

You are a raptor on the Front Range, bare-scaled and alone, guarding a small heap of glowing seeds against everyone else doing the same. That arrangement has a name, and it is eating the valley.

How to play

1 Press 2 for the stream, then hold the left mouse button. A ribbon of light throws from your claws. Sweep it over grey, cracked ground — life chases the end of it. That is how you earn coherence, and it is the whole early game.
Press 1 for claws when you want to break blocks instead, and 3–9 for materials to build with.
2 Press V to call the flock. Raptors who trust you fall in behind and follow you around. The more coherence you have, the more of them will come.
3 Find a Moloch and hold the stream on him. It does not hurt him — nothing does. It tethers him, and one stream is never enough. Your flock throw their streams too. Three at once and he is taken.

There is a line at the top of the screen that always names the next thing to do. If you are ever lost, read it and do that.

Hyperstition — the other way to win

A hyperstition is a fiction that makes itself true by being acted upon. You declare a future that is not real yet, and it becomes real only if enough others behave as though it already is.

H Speak it. Press J to cycle through the declarations — The Ground Returns, Preening, The Weave That Catches, up to The Golden Seed — then H to speak the one you have selected. The bar along the bottom shows them all and what each does. Some need coherence; one is spoken at a Moloch. Type /declare your own words in chat to word it yourself.
Y Align with one. A hyperobject appears in the sky above the Moloch, huge and barely visible — it is not real yet. Anyone nearby presses Y to align with it. Each raptor may align once. When enough have, the claim becomes true, the hyperobject solidifies, and it binds the Moloch outright.

This is the only commitment mechanic in the game. Everything that needs more than one raptor — healing a valley, bridging a gap, binding a Moloch, planting the Golden Seed — is a declaration someone speaks and others make true.

So: streams hold one Moloch by brute co-presence, in the same second. Declarations take longer to set up but need only agreement.

Controls

1–9 picks what is in your hands. Slot 1 is your claws (hold left mouse to break blocks), slot 2 is the stream, and 3–9 are materials you can place with right mouse. Scroll or Q/E cycles.
